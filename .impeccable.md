## Design Context

### Users
**Who**: Women in tech applying to jobs in Mexico, frustrated by systemic pay gaps and uncertain about salary negotiation.

**Context**: Users arrive anxious and hopeful — they need concrete data to feel confident negotiating. They're often analyzing job postings during limited free time, possibly after work. The tool must reduce cognitive load, not add to it.

**Job to be done**: Get instant, actionable intelligence on job fit + salary data to feel empowered walking into interviews/negotiations.

**Emotional state**: Frustrated by inequality, hopeful for change, needs confidence boost.

### Brand Personality
**3 words**: Trustworthy, feminist, empowering

**Voice**: Professional but warm. Data-driven without being cold. Speaks directly to women's experiences in tech without being preachy.

**Emotional goals**: 
- Reduce anxiety around salary negotiation
- Build confidence through concrete data
- Validate frustration (pay gap is real) while providing agency

### Aesthetic Direction
**Visual tone**: Clean, professional interface with warm accent colors. Feminist tech aesthetic — not stereotypically "feminine" but intentionally rejecting cold corporate HR tool vibes.

**Colors**: Deep Purple (`#1a0533`), Electric Violet (`#7c3aed`), Rose Gold (`#e8b4b8`) — approved.

**Theme**: Both light and dark mode intentionally supported.

**Anti-references**: 
- Generic corporate HR/ATS platforms (cold, bureaucratic)
- Overly playful startup aesthetics (trivializes the pay gap issue)
- Data dashboards that feel clinical

**Reference feel**: Professional tools that balance data credibility with human warmth — think Stripe's clarity meets a supportive mentor's tone.

### Design Principles
1. **Data with empathy**: Present hard numbers (pay gaps, fit scores) in a way that validates feelings and builds confidence, not anxiety.

2. **Reduce cognitive load**: Users are already stressed. Every screen should have one clear primary action. Progressive disclosure for complexity.

3. **Actionable over comprehensive**: Don't just show problems — always provide next steps, scripts, or concrete actions.

4. **Trustworthy transparency**: Show sources (INEGI), explain methodology briefly, never hide uncertainty.

5. **Feminist tech aesthetic**: Warm but professional. Purple as primary (not blue). Rose gold as accent, not decoration. Design signals "this was made for us, not about us."

### Accessibility
- **Target**: WCAG AA compliance
- **Color contrast**: Ensure all text meets 4.5:1 minimum
- **Color blindness**: Don't rely solely on color to convey meaning (use icons, labels, patterns)
- **Motion**: Respect reduced-motion preferences for animations
